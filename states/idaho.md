# Rockhounding in Idaho

The **[Idaho Rockhounding Guide](https://usarockhounding.com/rockhounding-map/idaho/)** is the main state resource for mapped locations and trip research.

## Idaho rockhounding links
- [Idaho locations and map](https://usarockhounding.com/rockhounding-map/idaho/)
- [Public-land research](https://usarockhounding.com/rockhounding-map/public-land-rockhounding/)
- [Material explorer](https://usarockhounding.com/rockhounding-map/rocks-minerals-fossils/)
- [Rules and access](https://usarockhounding.com/rockhounding-map/rules-access/)

Check land status, mining claims where relevant, road conditions and seasonal closures before a field trip.
